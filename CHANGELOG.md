# Changelog

## 7.6

### New
- **Frostbite Peak**, a twelfth place: a snowy obby with ice shelves, drifting floes, falling icicles, a crumbling snow bridge and a trampoline cliff.
- Hats: Traffic Cone, Chef's Hat, Headphones, Wizard Hat, Frost Crown.
- Emotes: cheer, shrug, salute, flex.
- Badges: Summiteer, Speedrunner, Expressive, Party Animal, Hat Collector, Bolt Baron.
- Themes: Ocean, Arcade, Frost, Lavender.
- Daily challenges for Frostbite Peak, a beat-the-clock run, Four Corners, Rocket Arena and an emote.
- "What's new" notes at the top of the Roadmap tab.

### Changed
- **Studio:** drag to lay or erase a row of bricks; Undo button and Ctrl+Z; bricks no longer stack on the same spot; brick count shown.
- **Battles:** health packs on every battle map; knockouts pay Tickets, with streak bonuses.
- **Economy:** limited items follow a daily market price, and collectors pay 90% of it in the item's own currency. The Earn tab lists every way to earn.
- **Earn tab:** the pretend card-payment form is gone. Ticket and Bolt packs show as "coming soon", and nothing asks for a card.

### Fixed
- Face maker crashed on every picture.
- "Create account" on the home page went back to Guest after a reload.
- "Test the save now" wrote to the wrong account slot.
- The currency exchange lost Tickets that weren't a whole ten.
- Plain and Studio obbies recorded every finish as 0.0s.
- Raiders in Build a Base and Defend never attacked the core.
- A daily challenge asked for a flag that Happy Home doesn't have.
- Keys stayed held after switching windows.
- Party Chat crashed when joining and could never send. It now follows parental controls and the chat filter.
- Safe-chat bot replies, and the Studio and Party Chat parental switches.
- Room codes were rejected by the relay. Over the public brokers, a guest never got the host's place, and could overwrite it.
- Data from other players, shared places and transfer codes is now fully checked. The online scoreboard could be injected with HTML.
- The Studio tutorial now matches the real toolbar.
